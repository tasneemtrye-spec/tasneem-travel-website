About Tasneem

Learn about Tasneem Travel and Tourism, a travel and tourism agency based in Taiz, Yemen, and its approach to clear travel enquiries.

Contact by emailContact details

Our vision

  • Make travel planning clearer and easier, from the first enquiry to reviewing available options.

Our mission

  • Organise travel information and make it easier for customers to ask questions and review details before deciding.

Our principles

  • Do not publish unverified prices or offers.
  • Do not claim licences or government approvals that have not been verified.
  • Explain that visa, permit and document decisions belong to the competent authorities.
  • Keep published contact details clear and consistent.

Our areas of interest

  • Flight and hotel enquiries and tourism programmes.
  • Hajj and Umrah enquiries subject to official licensing and seasonal requirements.
  • General information about visas, transport and travel documents.
