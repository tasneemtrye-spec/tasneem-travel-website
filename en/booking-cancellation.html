Booking, changes and cancellation

General information about booking enquiries, reviewing offers, and checking change, cancellation and refund terms before confirmation.

Contact by emailContact details

When is a booking confirmed?

  • After the service, price, availability and conditions have been reviewed and communicated.
  • After the customer agrees and the supplier's required booking steps are completed.
  • Keep the confirmation, ticket or voucher issued by the service provider.

Changes and cancellations

  • Flight ticket changes and cancellations depend on the airline and fare rules.
  • Hotel and tourism programme bookings follow their suppliers' policies.
  • Fees and deadlines vary by booking and provider.

Refunds

  • There is no single refund rule for every service.
  • The written offer should explain refundable amounts, fees and expected processing steps before confirmation.
  • Refunds depend on supplier terms and the approved payment method.

Before you pay

  • Request a written offer listing the service, price, taxes and fees.
  • Review cancellation, no-show and change conditions.
  • Do not transfer money to an unverified account or channel.
Note: Operational draft. No approved schedule of cancellation fees, refund percentages, refund periods or payment details has been supplied, so this page intentionally does not publish specific financial promises.
