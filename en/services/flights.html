Travel information and enquiries

Flight booking enquiries

Send us your route, travel dates and passenger details. We can review available flight options and explain fare, baggage and ticket conditions before you decide. Availability and final pricing depend on the airline and supplier at the time of enquiry.

Ask on WhatsAppContact details

Enquire about flight tickets from Yemen, routes from Aden where available, Yemenia flight options, baggage and ticket conditions.

What to include

  • Departure city and destination.
  • Travel dates and flexible date options.
  • Number of passengers and children's ages, if relevant.
  • Preferred airline, baggage needs and other requirements.

Flight enquiries

  • Domestic and international routes, subject to availability.
  • Enquiries about schedules and routes, including departures from Aden where listed.
  • You may state a preferred carrier, including Yemenia, for an availability check.
  • Ticket changes and cancellations depend on fare rules and airline conditions.

Before confirming

  • Check passenger names against travel documents before ticket issuance.
  • Review baggage, taxes, fees, change and refund conditions.
  • A website enquiry is not a confirmed booking or an issued ticket.
Important: Mentioning an airline, including Yemenia, does not imply an agency appointment, partnership or endorsement.

Frequently asked questions

Can I ask for a fare before booking?

Yes. Send your route, dates and passenger count so available options can be reviewed.

Can a ticket be changed or cancelled?

Changes and cancellations depend on the airline, fare type and applicable fees.
