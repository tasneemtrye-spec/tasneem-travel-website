Hajj and Umrah enquiries from Yemen

You may send an enquiry about Umrah from Yemen, seasonal Hajj programmes, accommodation and transport. Any registration or arrangement is subject to the licensing, seasonal rules and authorised providers recognised by the competent authorities.

Ask on WhatsAppContact details

Enquire about Hajj and Umrah programmes from Yemen, Umrah visa requirements, accommodation and transport, subject to official rules and authorised providers.

Enquiry topics

  • Individual, family and group Umrah programme enquiries.
  • Hajj programme information and official registration dates.
  • Accommodation and transport information within an authorised programme.
  • Published document and seasonal requirements.

Information to provide

  • Number of travellers and suitable travel period.
  • Departure city and preferred programme type.
  • Any accessibility or family requirements.

Before registration or payment

  • Verify the organiser's licence and seasonal authorisation.
  • Request written details of price, accommodation and transport.
  • Do not send passport images or sensitive identity details through the general enquiry form.
Important: This page does not claim that the agency holds a Hajj or Umrah licence. Any service delivery must be verified against the relevant season and official authorisations.

Frequently asked questions

Is registration open all year?

Hajj and Umrah dates and procedures depend on official seasonal instructions.

Does an enquiry guarantee a visa or permit?

No. Visa, permit and registration decisions are made by the competent authorities.
