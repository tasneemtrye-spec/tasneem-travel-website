Travel information and enquiries

Guidance on official travel documents

We can receive general questions about travel documents and publicly available procedures. This is informational guidance only; it does not mean the agency issues government documents or represents a government authority.

Ask on WhatsAppContact details

Ask for general information about passport, electronic identity card and travel document requirements. Official documents are issued by government authorities.

Topics you may ask about

  • General passport requirements and published procedures.
  • Electronic personal identity card information.
  • Documents commonly requested for travel enquiries.
  • How to locate the relevant official authority or platform.

Service boundaries

  • General information and initial guidance where available.
  • The agency does not issue passports or identity cards.
  • Representation or follow-up on another person's behalf requires lawful authority and a valid delegation, where permitted.

Protect your information

  • Do not send passport images or identity numbers through the general enquiry form.
  • Use official government portals when submitting documents.
  • Check official instructions before handing over original documents.
Important: Published requirements, fees and appointments may change. Confirm them with the competent government authority.

Frequently asked questions

Does the agency issue passports or identity cards?

No. Issuance and approval are the responsibility of the competent government authorities.

Can I attach a passport scan to the enquiry form?

No. Do not upload sensitive identity documents through the general enquiry form.
