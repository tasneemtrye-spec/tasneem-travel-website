Tasneem Travel and Tourism

Privacy notice

Understand what information the Tasneem website enquiry form asks for and how it is used to review and respond to service enquiries.

Contact by emailContact details

Information you may submit

  • Full name, telephone number and an alternative WhatsApp number.
  • Email address, country and city.
  • Service type, destination, travel date and passenger count.
  • Preferred contact method, message and consent to be contacted.

Why the information is requested

  • To understand and review the enquiry.
  • To contact the person who submitted the request.
  • To provide information about the requested travel or service enquiry.

How enquiries are processed

  • The website currently uses Netlify Forms to receive form submissions.
  • The hosting and form provider may process information needed to deliver the form service.
  • The website does not publish submitted enquiry details as public pages.

Do not send sensitive documents

  • Do not send passport scans, identity documents, passwords, one-time codes or payment card details through the general enquiry form.
  • Use official government portals when submitting documents to public authorities.

Privacy requests and outstanding policy details

  • Contact the published email or telephone number to ask about information you submitted or request a correction.
  • The agency still needs to confirm its retention period, deletion process and any additional data processors before this notice can be treated as final.
Note: Operational draft for review. The retention period, processing location, deletion procedure and designated privacy contact have not yet been confirmed.
